FAQS

What you see is exactly what you pay. We’ve streamlined the process so there are no surprise fees or upsells — just honest, transparent pricing based on your property. If you and your neighbor have the same size lawn, you’ll pay the same. Simple as that.
We currently cover the San Antonio metro area. Enter your address in the app to confirm availability.
Cash back is added to your Lawnify Wallet within 48 hours of each monthly payment. You can apply it toward future services and invoices instantly.
You can pause, cancel, or switch plans anytime directly in the app — no commitments, no fees, and no hassle, as long as changes are made at least 48 hours before your next scheduled visit.
We charge per visit, so you only pay for the times we actually service your lawn. To make payments easier, all visits are added to your account and billed together at the end of each month.
